If you don't know how to extract a listfile from an MPQ file, it's easy. Open the archive with MPQEditor Ladiks or a similar program, and at the root of the MPQ you'll see a file named “(listfile)”. That's the one—extract it and rename it to match the patch name. (If it comes from patch-0.mpq, name it patch-0.txt.)
